ChickCurio is a personal food diary for testing one suspect food at a time. Mark each day clean or flare until that food clears or busts.

• Test one food at a time on the day board (One suspect)
• Pick a food by typing a name, choosing a shelf chip, or scanning a barcode
• Mark today Clean or Flare; seven clean days clear it, a flare busts it
• Rotate off a busted food, then pick the next one
• Log food on the open day with no calorie count
• Tag place on a flare as Head, Gut, Skin, or Joints
• Read the ledger for Failed foods, Cleared foods, and Rotated off
• Export ledger as a shareable text summary
• Settings keeps marks on this device, with Replay intro, Cited notes, Contact, and Erase diary

A flare note is a personal log, not a diagnosis. Open Cited notes for NHS food intolerance and MedlinePlus food allergy links.
